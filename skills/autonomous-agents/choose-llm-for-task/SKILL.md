# Choose LLM For Task

Find the task type in the routing table below. Take the top-3 picks **in order** — try 1st, degrade to 2nd/3rd only if that harness is capped, unavailable, or scarce (see budgets). Cross-check the per-model cheat sheet and rules of thumb before committing to a scarce or costly pick.

**Claude Code**

## Rules of thumb

- Prefer free/flat-rate lanes (GLM-5.3 via Pi, OpenCode cheap models) before spending on metered API (Grok CLI, Gemini) or scarce quota.
- Light scouts, lookups, scaffolds: **Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna are the two scout models.** Under 100k tokens, Haiku 5.5 at medium. At or over 100k, or a growing agent history, Luna. Non-sensitive mass work stays on Muse Spark. Non-sensitive tool-calling subagents go to Muse Ember at max, not to a scout. Live-source scouting stays on Grok CLI. Do not spend OpenCode Go quota to reach Haiku or Luna.
- For live or recent-source work (what's new, who said it, which docs/posts to trust): **Grok CLI + grok-4.7, effort high.** Web + X search is the differentiator — scope there, then hand off. xhigh burns ~81k out/task and can cost more than gpt-6.2-astra xhigh (~$2.52). Do not use Luna for this lane; it does not search X.
- Escalate exactly one tier after a cheaper pick fails twice — don't jump straight to the most expensive model on first friction. A short Haiku 5.5 scout fails twice → gpt-6.2-sol at medium, not Haiku max. Luna fails twice → gpt-6.2-sol at medium, not high and not Astra. Terra medium fails twice on computer use, screenshots, or volume drafting → Terra max, not Sol and not Astra. Sol medium fails twice on coding → high, not xhigh (TB4 drops), not max, and not Terra. Sol high fails twice on coding → max. Sol fails on science, security, or review → gpt-6.2-astra at xhigh, not max. Astra xhigh fails twice → Fable 5.2 at high, not Astra max and not Fable max. Opus 5.5 is the long-context and writing pick, not the next Astra tier. Sonnet 5.5 max is not a cheaper Opus. A capped Astra+Opus hard task → one Kimi K3.1 Pro shot, not a K3 retry. Do not send security work to Fable 5.2. Do not send exploit-dev to 6.2 prod.
- Cross-vendor deliberately for reviews and second opinions (different training data/blind spots catch different bugs).
- Never use MiMo-V2.5 for long agentic sessions — reproducible mid-response cutoff bug. V2.6 Pro/Flash are candidates only; treat the cutoff as still open until rechecked.
- Muse Spark 1.3 is frontier-strong (AA 61) *and* the cheapest confirmed OpenCode lane — use it for non-sensitive bulk and everyday coding when region allows. **Contributor means prompts/inputs will likely be used for training. Never send secrets, private IP, or sensitive data.** Limited regions. Muse Ember at max is the non-sensitive tool-calling subagent (index 36, $0.14/task); it is not Spark and not an extraction model. Ember's training terms are unverified — still no secrets. Sensitive fallback is GPT-6 Luna. No-data-sharing OpenCode fallback is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash or LongCat-2.0. Beam Free is unlimited and weak (AA 30) — mechanical only.
- Gemini API costs real dollars. Default to Luna or DeepSeek V4.1 Flash for text volume. Use Hydrogen (pin high) when the task needs audio/video or a Gemini agent. Use 3.8 Flash when you need its ~300 tok/s or the lower $0.75/$3.75 rate, including search grounding. Computer use on Codex quota goes to Terra, not Hydrogen and not 3.8 Flash. Carbon is the hard Google shot during the $2/$10 promo (through 30 Nov); after that it is $4/$20 and ~2× the task cost. Do not start new work on Argon. Flash-Lite is no longer the cheapest extraction tier.
- OpenCode's scarce models (Kimi K3.1 Pro, Gemini 4 Argon, Step 5 Preview, Qwen3.8 Max, Grok, GLM-5.3, Haiku 5.5, GPT-6 Luna) are cheaper to reach through their native harness when one exists: GLM-5.3 via Pi, Grok 4.7 via Grok CLI, Haiku via Claude Code, Luna via Codex, Hydrogen/Carbon/Argon via the Gemini API. Hydrogen and Carbon were not confirmed on Go as of 13 Nov. Save Go for models unique to it (Muse Spark, Muse Ember, Beam Free, Hy4, GLM-5.3-Flash, Qwen3.8 Flash, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, MiMo V2.6, MiniMax M3.1 Flash, Kimi K3.1 Pro, Step 5 Preview). Ember's Go quota was not remeasured on 18 Nov.
- Hard work: gpt-6.2-astra at xhigh first. Opus 5.5 when the prefix will pass 272k, the task is writing, or Codex is capped. Fable 5.2 at high when Astra xhigh is not enough — not Fable max and not Astra max. Kimi K3.1 Pro for one scarce agentic shot when those are capped. Carbon is the paid Google shot while its promo holds. Computer use is Terra, not Astra. Everyday coding is Sol at medium, not Astra. Do not send hard refactors to Sonnet 5.5 max, to Terra, or to Fable max. Do not spend K3 quota. Do not send security work to Fable 5.2 or exploit-dev to 6.2 prod.
- Pin a Codex model (`gpt-6.2-sol` at medium for coding, `gpt-6.2-astra` at xhigh for hard problems, science, review, and security review, `gpt-6.1-terra` at medium for computer use, screenshots, and volume drafting, `gpt-6-luna` for long or Codex-side scouts). Raise Sol to high only after medium misses twice. Raise Terra to max only after medium misses twice. Inside Claude Code, pin scouts to `claude-haiku-5-5` at medium. Pin the Codex model explicitly; an unset model may fall back to the flagship.
- Above 272k input, gpt-6.2-sol, gpt-6.2-astra, and gpt-6.1-terra price the whole API request at 2× input/cache and 1.5× output. Luna keeps a 272k cliff. Prefer Opus 5.5 / GLM for huge-prefix agents. Fable 5.2 shares Opus's $0.20 cache read and costs 2× on uncached input. Hydrogen has no 272k cliff and bills real dollars at $1.50/$7.50.
